Questions people ask.

Short answers to what most people want to know before they install.

Your data

Is my money data safe?

It stays in WealthOn’s private storage on your phone, which other apps can’t read, and Android encrypts the phone’s storage. It never goes to our servers. Add a fingerprint lock so nobody else can open the app, and a backup password so your backups are sealed with AES-256.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Send a copy to Google Drive in one tap whenever you like, and restore it on your new phone. Turn on automatic backup and a copy is saved by itself every week to a folder you choose (every day with Pro). Set a backup password and only you can open those files.

Does the AI see my transactions?

No. Smart categories are off until you say yes. Then they get only shop names you haven’t filed before; names that look like a person’s stay on your phone, and so do amounts, dates and account numbers. The coach gets monthly totals by your category names, only when you ask it. “Read with AI” (for statements WealthOn can’t read by itself) sends the statement’s lines only when you tap it, with card, account and IBAN numbers hidden first. Nothing about you is stored: the server only remembers which category common shop names (like Starbucks or Swiggy) belong to, so it can answer without asking the AI again. Settings → Your data, shown lists every request, word for word.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Settings has “Erase all data”, and uninstalling the app removes it too. We keep no copy to ask us to delete. Backups you saved yourself stay where you put them, and if your phone’s own Google backup is on, Android may include WealthOn in it; you control that in Android settings.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline, including Quick answers. Only the AI helpers (the coach, smart categories and reading a statement with AI) need a connection.

Using the app

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank that gives you a statement as a PDF, Excel (XLS or XLSX) or CSV file, which all of them do from their app, net banking or email. Locked PDFs open with their password, on your phone. Import one from each bank and they all land in one place. Scanned PDFs (pictures of paper) can’t be read; use the Excel or CSV download for those. Tested with statements from Kotak, HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Canara, PNB, Bank of Baroda, IndusInd, Federal, Paytm Payments Bank, Revolut, Chase and UK and German banks.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. It supports 20 currencies, and words follow where you live: “EMI” in India, “loan payment” elsewhere. Your month can start on the 1st or on your payday.

Can I have accounts in two currencies?

Yes, two at a time: your main currency and one more, for example rupees and euros. Mark an account, an entry, a repeat like your salary, or a whole statement as euros, and it shows in euros while your totals stay in rupees. The rate updates by itself every day (from the European Central Bank), or you can type your own.

Do I have to type every expense?

No. Import your statement once a month, and set rent, salary and other fixed amounts to repeat by themselves. Typing a single expense takes two taps.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. Your first statement import is sorted by AI for free, and you get one AI coach question every month. Nothing expires and there’s no subscription: buy Pro once when you want it every time.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. WealthOn is Android first, and an iPhone version is planned from the same app. Leave your email on the feedback page to hear when it’s ready.
