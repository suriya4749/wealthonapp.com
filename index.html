Questions people ask.

Short answers to what most people want to know before they install.

Your data

Is my money data safe?

It stays in a database on your phone and never goes to our servers. You can add a fingerprint lock so nobody else can open the app.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Send a copy to Google Drive in one tap whenever you like, and restore it on your new phone. With Pro, automatic backup saves a copy by itself every day or week to a folder you choose.

Does the AI see my transactions?

No. Smart categories get only shop names you haven’t filed before. The coach gets monthly totals by your category names. Nothing is stored, and you can turn both off.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Settings has “Erase all data”, and uninstalling the app removes it too. There is no copy anywhere else to ask us to delete.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline except smart categories and the coach, which need a connection.

Using the app

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank that lets you download a statement as Excel (XLS or XLSX) or CSV, which most do from their app or net banking. Import one from each bank and they all land in one place. PDF statements aren’t supported because they can’t be read reliably.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. It supports 21 currencies, and words follow where you live: “EMI” in India, “loan payment” elsewhere. Your month can start on the 1st or on your payday.

Do I have to type every expense?

No. Import your statement once a month, and set rent, salary and other fixed amounts to repeat by themselves. Typing a single expense takes two taps.

What happens when the Pro trial ends?

Nothing is lost. Pro features lock again and you carry on with Free. Buy Pro once if you want it back. There’s no subscription.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. WealthOn is for Android first. Write to us if you want to hear when an iPhone version is ready.
