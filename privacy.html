Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version

WealthOn has no accounts. Everything you enter stays in a database on your phone. We (the developer) never see it and keep no copy of it. Two optional smart features send a small amount of data to be processed and then forgotten. You can turn them off. WealthOn is made by Suriya Kumar, an independent developer in Dindigul, Tamil Nadu, India, who is responsible for this policy and for how the smart features handle data.

What the app stores on your phone

The money details you type in or import: income, expenses, loans and EMIs, budgets, savings, deposits, investments, goals and insurance policies, plus your settings and the name you give. They are stored only on this device.

Bank statement import

When you import a bank statement (a PDF, Excel or CSV file you choose), the file is read on your phone. Its contents are not uploaded anywhere. If the PDF is locked, its password is only used to open it on your phone and is not saved. You check every entry before anything is saved, and you can remove an import later.

Optional smart features (off until you agree)

• Smart categories. Off until you say yes, and only with WealthOn Pro. The first time you import a bank statement with Pro, WealthOn asks before sending anything. If you agree, the names of shops and payees WealthOn has not seen before and your category names are sent to our processing service, which suggests a category for each one. Payee names that look like a person’s (a transfer to a plain name with no shop word) are kept on your phone and not sent. No amounts, dates, account numbers or bank lines are sent.

• AI coach. If you ask the AI coach and agree, a summary of the month and the 3 months before is sent, with any question you type: totals by your category names, budget, EMIs, savings, goals and insurance cover. No shop or people names, no bank lines and no account numbers are sent.

• Exchange rate. Only if you use a second currency with “Update the rate by itself” on: about once a day, the two currency codes (for example EUR and INR) are sent to get today’s rate. Nothing about you or your money is sent.

This data goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a processing service we run on Cloudflare Workers. It passes the request to one AI provider to write the answer: Groq first, then a backup (Cloudflare Workers AI, Cerebras, OpenRouter, GitHub Models or NVIDIA) if that one is busy. We only use providers that state they do not train on or keep these requests (on OpenRouter, only ones that do not collect data). It is used only to produce the answer, is not stored by us, is not linked to you and is not used to train AI models. You can turn Smart categories on or off at any time in Settings; the AI coach only runs when you ask it.

How your data is protected

Your entries are kept in WealthOn’s private storage on the phone, which other apps can’t read, and Android encrypts the phone’s storage (all phones launched with Android 10 or later). App lock adds a fingerprint or face check before WealthOn opens. Backups: you can set a backup password in Settings → Backup password. Backup files are then sealed with AES-256 (the key is made from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256) and can’t be opened by anyone without it, including us. The password is kept in Android’s secure storage (Keystore) on this phone and is never sent anywhere. Without a backup password, a backup file is plain text that anyone who has the file can read.

What else leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to. When you tap "Back up to Google Drive" or "Export CSV", WealthOn creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example your own Google Drive). That copy is then governed by the service you picked. If Android's own device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include WealthOn's data in that backup, as it does for other apps; you control this in Android settings.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data. You don't need an internet connection to use the app; only the optional smart features use it.

Permissions

Notifications: for the reminders you keep on (EMIs and bills, premiums, deposit maturities, money lent or borrowed, and backups). Folder access: only if you turn on automatic backup; WealthOn then writes its backup file only to the folder you choose. Biometrics: only if you turn on App lock; the fingerprint or face check is done by Android and WealthOn never receives it. Vibration: for haptic feedback. Internet: only for the optional smart features.

Deleting your data

Deleted items stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days, then are removed. Settings → Erase all data removes everything immediately. Uninstalling the app also removes all data from the phone. Data sent to the smart features is not stored, so there is nothing to delete there.

Children

WealthOn is meant for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children.
